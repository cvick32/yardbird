#+TITLE: Restrict existential prerequisites to enabling conditions
#+DATE: 2026-11-12

The existential-action requirement extension now projects the validated witness
body onto source-state conditions. Next-state update atoms are replaced by true.
Conjunctions/disjunctions retain their structure; implications and conditionals
retain their guards only when those guards depend on the source state. Mixed
negations and future-state guards are discarded whole. This is a weakening of
the observed body, never an asserted lemma.

Declared witness applications remain opaque action parameters: their captured
future-state terms do not turn an enabling read into an update. Captures are
preserved literally. Extraction shares the existing trace work budget.

512 library tests pass. The paired-encoding regression and independent Z3
selector-case equivalence check pass. A focused additional assertion checks that
the existential fixture retains precisely its enabling read and opaque witness,
while removing both the array equality and quantified update helper.

Seven sequential, unprofiled benchmark runs use the standard release build,
immediate guidance, work 4096, predecessor-first transition lookup, and
when-unproductive prerequisite guidance. All inputs are the generated German
and Chandra VMT files from quantified-transition-systems. Primary timeout: 10 s,
external grace: 1 s; depth 5 means checks 0--4.

|---+---+---+---+---|
| German | 2/5 | 5/5 | 3.131 | 3/3 |

German times: 3.178, 3.128, 3.131 seconds. Chandra times: 8.000, 8.356,
7.954 seconds. Chandra's previous median was 7.902 seconds; no speedup is claimed.
German also completes with the matched 120-second allowance in 3.130 seconds;
whole-body tracing previously reached only 3/5 checks within that allowance.
This resolves the measured German regression and recovers bounded depth-five
coverage on both targets. It is not an unbounded safety proof or a win over Z3.
