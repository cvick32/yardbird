* What the measurements establish
1. The default losses are overwhelmingly outside solver checks. Across its 18
   timed-out cases, raw solver time is 0.24–1.73% of wall time, median 0.58%.
   Refinement overhead and selection quality are the first optimization targets.
2. All 20 source VMTs have quantified read equations and no store applications.
   Generated frame/update conditions place the semantic work in quantifiers.
   Native arrays alone complete only 4/20, native quantifiers complete 19/20.
3. Guidance and batch 20 recover Peterson, Peterson-two-process, and ticket,
   and speed up MESI. They do not repair the broad lock-server/consensus losses.
   Guidance changes both scheduling and winners (20), so its improvement over
   default must not all be attributed to trace-generated instances.
4. Bakery is sensitive to capture selection: source captures alone close a large
   gap; adding read-frontier guidance loses the benefit. MESI also regresses at
   the read-frontier step, before read support or property reuse is added.
5. Native-quantifier hybrid helps multisig: 0.567s versus native Z3's external
   timeout, with four of five Z3 checks complete. Database chain replication
   times out under both baselines with four completed checks.

* Profiles and concrete code leads
Profiles are separate five-second diagnostic runs; timers are nested and must
not be summed as disjoint costs. Profiling changes runtime, so use the unprofiled
screen and repeated confirmation for performance comparisons.

- lockserv/default: 5.04s driver work, 4.67s strategy_sat, 0.036s raw solver;
  0.944s binder preparation, 0.715s extractor initialization, 0.736s dependency
  discovery, 0.581s graph expansion. 88 solver checks, 86 model preparations.
  Both default and batch20 have a median productive batch size of one.
- lockserv/guided: zero instances selected directly by CountermodelCandidates;
  128 unsupported predecessor trace frontiers at holds_lock frames 1/2.
  client_server_ae likewise selects zero trace instances and also stops at an
  opaque quantified helper. Quantified-equation fallback exists, so this is an
  incomplete/inefficient handoff, not absence of all support for such updates.
- MESI/guided: 20 solver checks and 0.366s strategy_sat. Full guided stack:
  57 checks, 4.70s strategy_sat and only two completed depths in the profile.

Recommended implementation order (hypotheses until patched and measured):

1. Preserve intact source capture tuples as a focused candidate-selection change.
   Start with source_captures.rs::preferred. It selects an entire source helper
   argument tuple compatible with model eclasses and respects literal bindings.
   The bakery experiment provides direct evidence for the existing opt-in.
   Expand testing before making it a global default.
2. Diagnose/readjust read-frontier priority using bakery and MESI as regression
   targets. refinement.rs::discover_read_frontiers prioritizes demanded requests
   ahead of ordinary candidates. Gate work using measured usefulness and retain
   fair ordinary search; merely increasing trace work is poorly supported here.
3. Reduce model-independent rebuilding. QuantifierPlan::prepare (mod.rs:448)
   recollects/sorts/translates subterms and constructs catalogs; candidate_builder.rs
   constructs an extractor per nonempty batch, whose source loop reparses and
   recosts terms (extractor.rs:150). Cache immutable syntax/catalogs per problem
   or depth; retain proper invalidation of model eclasses, evaluations and matches.
4. Prefer relevant bundles of quantified transition/frame conditions. The effort
   policy returns after the first productive operation (effort.rs:558); raising
   the per-rule winner cap alone still yields mostly singleton productive batches.
   Test bounded, demand-related bundles, not indiscriminate cross-rule batching.
5. Make quantified read-predecessor lookup more direct. array/countermodel.rs:111
   follows whole-array update paths and stops unsupported at line 201. Reuse the
   existing quantifier equation/request machinery to index target read equations
   and propagate exact source captures through helper/guard conditions. Never
   assert model equalities or skip instance validation.

AST-size cost, no-unroll-on-loop and prefer-axioms (all with batch20) were also

profiling: none completed. AST-size gained partial depth on lockserv and bakery,
but these results do not support a general fix through those settings alone.

* Frontier regression profile
The paired five-second profiles isolate the transition in behavior:
- Bakery: captures completes in 14 solver checks; frontiers reaches the timeout
  after 49 checks, selecting 14 demanded-index instances but discovering zero
  traced-equation requests. Captures selects 13 ordinary binder instances,
  frontiers 121. This supports a candidate-priority regression, not just timer cost.
- MESI: captures completes in 20 checks; frontiers times out after 60 checks,
  with 791 demanded-index requests attempted and 23 instances selected, but zero
  traced-equation requests discovered. Ordinary binder selections rise 27 to 88.
- Peterson benefits slightly from frontiers in this diagnostic; gating should
  preserve that opportunity rather than treating frontier guidance as universally bad.
Raw counters and both complete profiles are retained in frontier-profiles/.
